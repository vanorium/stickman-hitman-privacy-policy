# stickman-hitman-privacy-policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vanorium ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and processes information about users ("you", "your") playing the game Stickman Hitman (the "Game").
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. By using the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
1. Information We Collect
We collect data to analyze gameplay, improve game mechanics, and ensure performance stability. The information we collect is not directly personal information.     
This data includes:
Gameplay Analytics: Your progress (e.g., completed levels), in-game purchases (acquired skins), time spent in the game, and interaction history with game interfaces.
Technical Information: Device type and model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, system language, and automated crash reports.
2. How We Use the Information
The collected data is used exclusively for internal development and support of the Game:
To identify and fix bugs, glitches, and crashes.
To analyze level difficulty and balance gameplay mechanics.
To understand which features and in-game items (such as skins) are most popular.
To generally improve the overall quality of the Game and user experience.
3. Third-Party Services (GameAnalytics)
To collect and process gameplay statistics, we use a third-party analytical service called GameAnalytics.
GameAnalytics automatically receives access to the technical and gameplay data described in Section 1. The processing of this data is subject to their own privacy policy. We highly recommend reviewing it on their official website:
GameAnalytics Privacy Policy
4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with third parties. Exceptions are limited to:
Integrated analytical services (GameAnalytics) necessary to maintain and evaluate the Game.
Compliance with legal obligations, if requested by authorized government or law enforcement agencies.
5. Data Security
We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
6. Children's Privacy
Stickman Hitman does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately.
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective immediately upon being posted on this page. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.
